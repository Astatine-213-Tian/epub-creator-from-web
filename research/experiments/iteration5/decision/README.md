The v1 modules preserve the initial blind benchmark. The v2 preparer builds the
final reference-anchored packets, the shared rating runner executes anonymous
style and semantic panels, and the v2 analyzer computes source-level outcomes.
Prompt and schema assets are under `assets/`.

was best at 6/8 sources, below the formal 7/8 gate, while the LoRA achieved 0/3
complete book successes.
